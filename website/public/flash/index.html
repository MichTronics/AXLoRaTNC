AXLoRaTNC

AX.25 packet-radio TNC for ESP32 + LoRa
AX.25 Level 2 connected mode • KISS • WA8DED • APRS • NET/ROM

1 Choose channel

2 Select your board

3 Connect & flash

Plug your board into USB. Use Chrome or Edge — Web Serial is not supported in Firefox or Safari.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge.

4 First use

  1. Open a serial monitor at 115 200 baud.
  2. Set your callsign: callsign N0CALL-0
  3. Check radio defaults: radio
  4. Attach a KISS client (kissattach, Dire Wolf, BPQ) or switch to WA8DED: mode ded
